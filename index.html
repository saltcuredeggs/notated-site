Notated: Slides

Do the work. Boards for your projects and notes, kept on your device unless you choose to sync.

Notated: Slides lays out each project as a board of cards, grouped into categories. Each card opens into a rich-text note where you can add images, links, and audio. A to-do list sits alongside, so what you need to do stays next to what you are working on.

  • Works fully on your device, with no account needed.
  • Your boards and notes stay on your device unless you create a free account to sync them across your devices.
  • Signing out stops syncing and keeps your boards on that device.
  • Delete your account and everything synced to it from inside the app.
  • Choose from several color themes.
