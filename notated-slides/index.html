Notated: Slides

Do the work. A board for your projects and notes that runs on your device, with optional sync.

Notated: Slides lays out your projects as boards of cards, grouped into categories. Each card opens into a rich-text note where you can add images, links, and audio. A to-do list sits alongside, so what you need to do stays next to what you are working on.

  • Works fully on your device, with no account needed.
  • Optional sync across your devices with a free account.
  • Choose from several color themes.
  • Delete your account and everything synced to it from inside the app.
