Notated: Slides privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

In short: Your boards and notes stay on your device unless you turn on sync. If you do, I store your email address and your synced content on a server so it can appear on your other devices. You can delete your account and everything synced to it from inside the app at any time.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Notated: Slides app, made by the independent developer behind notated.space ("I" or "me"). Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

If you do not use sync

Your projects, cards, notes, to-do items, and media are stored only on your device. I do not receive them, and you do not need an account to use the app.

If you turn on sync

Sync is optional. To use it, you create an account with an email address and a password. When sync is on, I collect and store:

  • Your email address and password. The password is handled by the authentication service described below and is stored in hashed form, not as plain text.
  • Your content, so it can be copied to your other devices. This includes your projects, categories, cards and their notes, to-do items, and any images, audio, and links you add.

I use this information only to run your account and to sync your content. I do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to track you.

Where your data is stored

Synced data is stored with Supabase, a hosting and authentication provider that acts as my service provider. Data travels between the app and Supabase over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Supabase's handling of data is covered by its privacy policy.

Signing out and deleting your account

Signing out keeps everything on your device and stops syncing. It does not delete anything stored on my server.

Deleting your account is available inside the app, and you confirm it by entering your password. It permanently removes your account and everything synced to it from my server, including your uploaded images and audio. This cannot be undone, and there is no grace period.

When you delete your account, the app also asks whether to remove this device's copy of your content. The choice is yours. If you have the app on other devices, their local copies are not removed remotely, so delete them there too if you want them gone.

If you cannot use the app to delete your account, email support@notated.space from the address you signed up with and I will remove it.

What I do not do

  • I do not sell or rent personal data.
  • I do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

Children

Notated: Slides is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above.
