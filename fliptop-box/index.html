FlipTop Box

Save recipes from anywhere, file them into colored tins, cook one step at a time, and plan your week.

FlipTop Box is a personal recipe collection for iPhone and iPad. Save recipes from websites, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, screenshots, and scanned cookbook pages. Read them as a clean magazine-style page, then cook with a full-screen, one-step-at-a-time view and timers.

  • File recipes into colored tins.
  • Plan meals across the week.
  • Scan cookbook pages with your camera.
  • No account and no subscription. Your recipes can sync across your devices through your own iCloud.
